# Vibe Coding Project Ideas — Zodiac Theme

## 1. Zodiac Trivia Quiz
*Genre: Quiz*
Timed multiple-choice questions about zodiac lore and traits, with a score tracker and streak bonus for consecutive correct answers.

## 2. Zodiac Memory Match
*Genre: Puzzle / Matching*
Classic flip-two-tiles game where the 12 pairs are zodiac symbols or character portraits instead of generic icons.

## 3. Sign Compatibility Checker
*Genre: Utility / Lookup*
Pick two signs from a dropdown, get a compatibility score and short blurb pulled from a 12x12 lookup table.

## 4. Element Team Sorter
*Genre: Puzzle / Drag-and-drop*
Drag each of the 12 signs into their correct element (Fire/Earth/Air/Water), with a synergy blurb once a full team is sorted.

## 5. Zodiac Character Collector
*Genre: Collection / Gacha*
Pull or unlock one of the 12 signs at a time, revealing their class (mage/swordsman) and bonded spirit beast, saved to a running collection screen.

## 6. Spirit Beast Idle Trainer
*Genre: Idle / Clicker*
Pick a sign and their bonded spirit beast, then let stats (bond level, strength) tick up passively over time, with occasional taps to speed things along.

## 7. Bonding Visual Novel
*Genre: Narrative / Visual Novel*
A short branching story where dialogue choices with a spirit beast or classmate affect an affinity stat and lead to different short endings.

## 8. Class Trial Battle
*Genre: Turn-based Combat / RPG*
1v1 fight between a mage and a swordsman sign, each with a different move set (spell vs strike) and simple HP bars.

## 9. Walkable Academy Hub
*Genre: Exploration / Adventure*
Move a character around a top-down town, walking up to buildings (Academy Hall, Stables, Archives, Market) to see what each one does.
